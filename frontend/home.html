Streetwatch is a shared, visible log of local infrastructure hazards — potholes, broken streetlights, flooding, damaged sidewalks — reported by the people who actually walk past them every day.

How it works

01

Spot it

See a pothole, broken light, or flooded stretch of road on your way somewhere.

02

Pin it

Log in, drop a pin on the map, describe the hazard, submit.

03

Track it

Everyone can see the report's status move from Reported to Resolved.
