SDG 11

Sustainable Cities and Communities

UN Sustainable Development Goal 11 calls for cities that are safe, resilient, and inclusive. A big part of that is infrastructure that actually works — roads without hazards, lighting people can rely on, drainage that doesn't flood the first street it meets. Streetwatch gives communities a direct, visible channel to flag what's broken and see it get fixed, instead of hazards sitting unreported and unresolved indefinitely.
